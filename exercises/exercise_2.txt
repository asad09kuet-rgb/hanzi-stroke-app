# Exercise 2 · Lesson 4 · Paragraph

他是美国留学生。 | He is an American overseas student.
他是我的同学，我们一起上课，一起学习汉语。 | He is my classmate; we go to class together and study Chinese together.

# Exercise 2 · Lesson 4 · Dialogues
你是留学生吗？ | Are you an overseas student?
是，我是留学生。 | Yes, I am an overseas student.
你学习什么？ | What do you study?
我学习汉语。你呢？ | I study Chinese. And you?
我也学习汉语。 | I study Chinese too.
你学习法语吗？ | Do you study French?
我不学习法语。 | I don't study French.
他是老师吗？ | Is he a teacher?
不是，他不是老师，他是学生。 | No, he isn't a teacher, he is a student.
你们的汉语老师是谁？ | Who is your Chinese teacher?
我们的汉语老师是张老师。 | Our Chinese teacher is Teacher Zhang.
