# Exercise 3 · Lesson 5 · Dialogues
你家有几口人？ | How many people are in your family?
我家有四口人。 | There are four people in my family.
爸爸、妈妈、哥哥和我。 | Dad, Mum, my older brother and me.
你爸爸做什么工作？妈妈呢？ | What does your dad do? And your mum?
我爸爸是律师，妈妈是医生。 | My dad is a lawyer and my mum is a doctor.
你哥哥做什么工作？ | What does your older brother do?
他不工作，他和我都是大学生。 | He doesn't work; he and I are both university students.
你们学习什么？ | What do you study?
我们学习汉语。 | We study Chinese.
你们班有多少学生？ | How many students are in your class?
二十五个。 | Twenty-five.
你们班有法国人吗？ | Are there any French people in your class?
有。 | Yes, there are.
有几个法国人？ | How many French people?
两个。 | Two.
